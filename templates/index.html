📖 How to Play

  • Choose your word length and click Start Game.
  • Guess the hidden English word within 10 attempts.
  • Rules: No duplicate letters allowed in guesses, no restricted words, and simple plurals (ending in 'S') are excluded.
  • Bull (🐂): A correct letter in the exact right spot.
  • Cow (🐮): A correct letter, but in the wrong spot.
💡 Example: If hidden word is KING and you guess MIND:
• 2 Bulls (letters I and N are right)
• 0 Cows (no other letters exist in KING)
